- the names, look, personality, facial expressions and movements of Imp and Sparky — in any form,
  including redrawn versions or versions in another style or medium;
- the code that draws and animates them: `src/renderer/common/rigs/` (all files);
- `resources/characters/` — the built-in characters;
- icons: `build/icon.png`, `resources/icons/`;
- the demo animation `docs/media/`;
- the scripts that generate these images: `scripts/generate-characters.mjs`,
  `scripts/generate-assets.mjs`.
